Privacy Policy

Updated September 24, 2026 · Terms of Use · Support · Français · All apps

Controller

Charles Venayre, contact: charlesvenayre@me.com. No account is created, no email address or phone number is asked for, and there is no connection to any bank.

What the app stores, and where

Your onboarding answers (first name, categories, estimated amounts, triggers, risky hours), your urges, your waiting items, your projects, your challenges and your settings are stored on your iPhone only. This version syncs nothing with iCloud: we hold no copy, and deleting the app or losing the phone erases the history, unless your iPhone is backed up. You can export your data as CSV and erase everything in Settings.

Subscription

Purchases go through the App Store. We see neither your name nor your payment method.

Notifications

All notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. None goes through a server.

Usage statistics

To understand what is used and what gets in the way, the app sends usage statistics: screens opened and how long, actions (urge recorded with its category and an amount bracket, resistance, purchase, review, challenge, project created, paywall shown, subscription), the app version, the iOS version, the iPhone model, the language, and an installation identifier created by the app and renewed at each reinstall. The country is derived from the IP address on the server, without the address being kept. Never sent: the exact amount, the title of an urge, the name of a project, your first name. These statistics are pseudonymous: tied to that installation identifier, never to a name, an e-mail address or an account, they cannot be used to recognise you. Purpose: to understand usage and improve the app. Retention: 13 months. Hosting: Supabase, European Union. No resale, no advertising, no cross-referencing with other services. No setting in the app turns them off: to object, write to us at charlesvenayre@me.com.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no data resale, no advertising profile, no connection to your bank.

Your rights

Erasing everything is done in Settings, without asking us. Deleting the app ends all collection. For any question or request about your data: charlesvenayre@me.com. Last updated: 24 September 2026.
