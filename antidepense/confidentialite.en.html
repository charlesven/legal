Privacy Policy

Controller

Charles Venayre, contact: charlesvenayre@me.com. No account is created, no email address or phone number is asked for, and there is no connection to any bank.

What the app stores, and where

Your onboarding answers (first name, categories, estimated amounts, triggers, risky hours), your urges, your waiting items, your projects, your challenges and your settings are stored on your iPhone only. This version syncs nothing with iCloud: we hold no copy, and deleting the app or losing the phone erases the history, unless your iPhone is backed up. You can export your data as CSV and erase everything in Settings.

Subscription

Purchases go through the App Store. We see neither your name nor your payment method.

Notifications

All notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. None goes through a server.

Usage statistics

To understand what is used and what gets in the way, the app sends usage statistics: screens opened and how long, actions (urge recorded with its category and an amount bracket, resistance, purchase, review, challenge, project created, paywall shown, subscription), the app version, the iOS version, the iPhone model, the language, and an installation identifier created by the app and renewed at each reinstall. If you installed the app from an App Store ad, the campaign and keyword the install came from are also sent, as provided by Apple. The country is derived from the IP address on the server, without the address being kept. Never sent: the exact amount, the title of an urge, the name of a project, your first name. These statistics are pseudonymous: tied to that installation identifier, never to a name, an e-mail address or an account, they cannot be used to recognise you. Purpose: to understand usage and improve the app. Retention: 13 months. Hosting: Supabase, European Union. No resale, no advertising, no cross-referencing with other services. No setting in the app turns them off: to object, write to us at charlesvenayre@me.com.

Ad measurement (Meta)

To know which Facebook or Instagram ad led you to the app, and to show fewer but better ones, the app may send a few events to Meta Platforms Ireland. This happens only under the following conditions.

  • Only if you accept it in Apple's "Tracking" prompt, shown after you have discovered the app: without your consent, nothing is sent to Meta.
  • What is sent to Meta Platforms Ireland: the device's advertising identifier, the app being opened, and the start of a trial or a subscription (product, amount, currency).
  • What is never sent to Meta: your urges, your amounts, your waiting items, your projects, your challenges, your reviews, your onboarding answers, your first name, and no banking data.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking: nothing more is sent from then on. Meta keeps this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Without your consent, only Apple's attribution (SKAdNetwork) tells Meta, with no identifier at all, that an install came from an ad.

Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Recipient: Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd (measurement of Facebook and Instagram ads, only with your consent). The usage statistics described above are never shared with Meta.

What we do not do

No ads inside the app, no data resale, no advertising profile, no connection to your bank. The only tracking across apps is the Meta ad measurement described above, only if you accepted it in Apple's "Tracking" prompt.

Your rights

Erasing everything is done in Settings, without asking us. Deleting the app ends all collection. For any question or request about your data: charlesvenayre@me.com. Last updated: 5 October 2026.
