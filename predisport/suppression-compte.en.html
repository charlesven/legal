Delete my Predisport account

App: Pêche en mer – Predisport (Android and iPhone) · Publisher: Charles Venayre

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and tap the profile icon at the top of Home.
  2. Scroll to Account › Delete account.
  3. Tap Send confirmation code: a 6-digit code is emailed to your account address.
  4. Enter the code and tap Delete permanently. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Without the app

Email contact@predisport.com from your account's email address with the subject "Predisport account deletion". We reply within 30 days and delete the account and its data within 30 days of verifying your identity.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, photo, bio), sessions, catches, spots, GPS traces, photos, messages, groups, friendships, preferences and thresholds, notification tokens, and the usage statistics linked to your account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Billing records (order id, amount, date): 10 years, legal obligation.
  • Technical logs: 12 months at most, not linked to your profile.
  • Already-aggregated pseudonymous usage statistics not linked to a person.

Your Google Play subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription: cancel it in Google Play (Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Predisport Pro) to stop being billed. Refunds are handled by Google.
