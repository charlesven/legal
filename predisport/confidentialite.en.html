Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Terms of Use · Français · Deutsch · Español · Italiano · Toutes les apps

This privacy policy describes how PrediSport (hereinafter "we", "our" or "the application") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable French data protection laws.

1. Data Controller

The data controller for personal data is PrediSport, an application published by Charles Venayre. For any questions regarding your data, you can contact us at:

contact@predisport.com

2. Data Collected

We collect the following categories of data:

2.1 Registration Data

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Password (encrypted)

2.2 Usage Data

  • Recorded fishing sessions (dates, durations, locations)
  • Created or saved spots
  • Recorded catches (species, sizes, weights, photos)
  • User preferences (weather thresholds, sport modes)
  • Pseudonymous usage statistics (screens viewed, actions performed, app and device version): details in section 2.6

2.3 Geolocation Data

  • GPS position during sessions (if authorized)
  • Spot locations
  • GPS trace of routes (if enabled)
  • Location for weather forecasts

2.4 Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system
  • Application version
  • Connection data and error logs

2.5 Payment Data

  • Payments are handled exclusively by Google Play (Google Play Billing)
  • We do not store any banking data
  • We only receive subscription status and expiration date

2.6 Usage statistics

  • What is sent: screens viewed, actions performed, app and device version, and a pseudonymous identifier linked to your session
  • What is never sent: your precise location, the content of your catches, sessions and messages, your photos, your email address
  • These statistics are pseudonymous: they are linked to your account when you are signed in, and are never sold or used for advertising
  • Retention period: 13 months
  • You can object to this collection by writing to contact@predisport.com: the statistics linked to your account are then deleted and no longer associated with it

3. Purposes of Processing

Your data is used to:

  • Provide and improve application features
  • Personalize your experience (forecasts, spot recommendations)
  • Manage your user account and subscriptions
  • Send push notifications (if authorized) and service emails
  • Ensure moderation of community content
  • Produce pseudonymous usage statistics to improve the service
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

Usage statistics are only used to measure how features are used and to diagnose malfunctions; they are never sold or used for advertising and are kept for a maximum of 13 months.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the type of data, processing is based on:

  • Performance of the contract (provision of service, account management)
  • Your consent (location, push notifications)
  • Our legitimate interest (service improvement, security, pseudonymous usage statistics)
  • Compliance with a legal obligation (retention of billing data)

5. Data Sharing

Your personal data is never sold to third parties. It may be shared with:

  • Supabase (hosting and database, servers in Europe)
  • OVH (web hosting, servers in France)
  • Google Play (in-app purchases, subscriptions and push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Open-Meteo, WorldTides, OpenFreeMap (weather, tide and map data — no personal data transmitted)

These providers are subject to contractual data protection obligations in compliance with the GDPR.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

Right of Access You may request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

Right of Rectification You may modify your personal information directly in the application or contact us to correct it.

Right to Erasure You may request the deletion of your account and all your data from the application settings.

Right to Data Portability You may request the export of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Right to Object You may object to the processing of your data for marketing purposes or on legitimate grounds.

Right to Restriction You may request the restriction of processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise these rights, contact us at:

contact@predisport.com

We respond to all requests within a maximum of 30 days.

In case of dispute, you may file a complaint with the CNIL (French National Commission for Information Technology and Civil Liberties): www.cnil.fr

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained for the following periods:

  • Account data: as long as the account is active, then 3 years after the last login
  • Session and catch data: as long as the account is active
  • Geolocation data: as long as the account is active
  • Billing data: 10 years (legal obligation)
  • Technical logs: 12 months maximum
  • Usage statistics: 13 months
  • Moderation data: 1 year after resolution

8. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

Encryption

  • HTTPS/TLS communication for all transmissions
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive data
  • Secure authentication tokens (JWT)

Access Control

  • Row Level Security policies on the database
  • Restricted and logged administrator access
  • No unauthorized third-party access

Data is hosted on European servers (Supabase EU, OVH France).

9. International Transfers

Your data is primarily stored and processed within the European Union. In the event that a transfer to a third country is necessary, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place (standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions).

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The application uses local storage technologies:

  • Local storage on the device: to save your preferences (language, units, thresholds, settings)
  • Authentication tokens: to maintain your active session
  • Local cache: for offline use of the application

The application does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers for commercial targeting purposes.

11. Children

The application is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with data, please contact us so we can delete it.

12. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy at any time. Changes will be published in the application and on our website. Your continued use of the service after publication of changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, contact us at:

contact@predisport.com
