Privacy Policy

Updated September 22, 2026 · Terms of Use · Support · Français · All apps

Controller

Charles Venayre, publisher of Envies. Contact: charlesvenayre@me.com. The Envies server is hosted by Supabase, in the European Union (Paris region).

No named account

Envies asks for no email address, no password and no sign-up, and connects to no social network. The first time you open it, the app opens an anonymous session: a technical identifier, created by the server, links your phone to your couple. It is attached to no identity and disappears with your account.

What you entrust to us

  • A display first name for you and one for your partner. You choose them freely; nothing obliges you to give real names.
  • Your couple's code, produced by the server, and the date the couple was created.
  • Your settings: maximum heat, excluded categories, card time, time zone, notification preferences.
  • Your game: the “yes” or “skip” answers given to each card, the wishes you have in common, the ones that are drawn, carried out or marked as favourites, and the matching dates.

Your individual answers are never shown to your partner: the server sends them only what you both accepted. Envies asks for neither your contacts, nor your location, nor access to your photos, and contains no messaging.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app registers your device's notification token, provided by Apple, to tell you about the evening draw and to let you know your partner has finished their cards. The content of a card or an individual answer never appears in a notification. The token is deleted as soon as you refuse notifications, change device or delete your account. Envies sends no notification about your trial, your subscription or a payment: those messages are Apple's business.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive a signed receipt from Apple, of which we keep the transaction identifier, the product purchased and the expiry date, in order to unlock the app for you (Solo) or for both of you (Duo). We receive neither your name, nor your address, nor your payment method.

Usage statistics

To understand what is used and what gets in the way, the app sends the screens you open and a few actions (card answered with its category, draw played, wish carried out, paywall shown, purchase). To these are added the app version, the device model, the system version, the language, the region and the time zone, along with an installation identifier renewed at each reinstall. Never the text of a card, a first name, the couple's code, or an answer tied to a specific card. No IP address is kept, no advertising identifier is used, no data is sold or shared with an advertiser, and Envies does not track your activity in other apps. These statistics are kept for 13 months and you can turn them off at any time in Us → Share usage statistics.

Your feedback

If you send us an idea or a problem from the Us tab, we receive your message, the screen concerned, the app version, the device model, the language and a short technical log, attached to your anonymous session. This feedback is kept for as long as it takes to deal with it.

Server technical log

The server records refusals (invalid invitation code, attempt quota exceeded) in order to detect abuse. This log contains the reason for the refusal and the session identifier, never the first name or the code that was typed. It is purged automatically.

Retention periods

Your game data is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account immediately erases your answers, your declarations, your first name, your recorded subscriptions and your anonymous session; if a step fails, the purge is resumed automatically within 24 hours. At the moment of deletion you choose whether the wishes you had in common are kept for your partner, to whom they also belong, or erased as well. When the last member of a couple leaves, the couple and its history disappear.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have a right of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability:

  • Access and portability: Us → Export my data, which returns all of your data in a JSON file.
  • Rectification: first names, heat and excluded categories can be changed at any time in Us.
  • Erasure: Us → Delete my account.
  • Objection: Us → Share usage statistics, to be turned off.

For any question or complaint: charlesvenayre@me.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority, or with the supervisory authority of your country.

Minors

Envies is for adults only. The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.
